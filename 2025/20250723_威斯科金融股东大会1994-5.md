She hid under the train seat during the whole journey to save money. 13岁的时候，为了保护她崭新的皮鞋，她赤脚步行了18英里，到了最近的火车站；然后搭乘了一辆去戈梅利的火车，为了省钱，整个旅程她躲在座位的下面。 After arriving there, the four-foot-ten-inch girl roamed the streets of Gomel, knocking on doors and asking for a job. “… I am not a beggar. And four years later, she married a shoe salesman, Isador Blumkin. But that same year, World War I broke out and she made up her mind. She sent her husband to America and started saving for herself. Then finally in 1916, she got her chance and boarded the Trans-Siberian Train to China. She rode through Manchuria (Yes, the place where Manchurian originated) to Tientsin, China, from where she boarded a boat to Yokohama, Japan. And finally, boarded Ava Maru, a cargo boat carrying peanuts from Yokohama to Seattle, United State of America. Thus covering 18,000 miles to reach America and three months of the tiring journey. 在16岁那年，她已经成功管理六名已婚男雇员。20岁的时候，她嫁给了鞋店推销员伊莎多·布罗姆金。同年，第一次世界大战爆发，她做出了一个决定，她送她的丈夫去美国，然后为她自己攒钱(前往美国的路费)。最终在1916年，她抓住了机会； 坐上了前往 X 国的西伯利亚铁路的火车，穿越满洲里，抵达了天津，从那里她坐上了一艘去日本横滨的船只，最终搭上了从横滨装运花生去美国西雅图的货轮”阿瓦丸”号，这场 18000 英里的长途旅行，整整耗费了三个月。 With the help of the Hebrew Immigrant Aid Society (HIAS), she could join her husband, Isadore. They soon shifted to Omaha, due to a significant number of Russian immigrants there. The primary reason being, she couldn’t read or write in English. 在希伯来移民援助协会的帮助下，她和她的丈夫伊莎多团聚，他们过了一阵子搬到了奥马哈，原因在于那里有大量的俄罗斯的移民，主要原因是，她不会读写英语。 “I was too dumb. They couldn’t drill it in me with a nail. She used to sell the furniture with a hair thing profit margin (e.g if she bought for 10,shewouldsellfor10.1), thus making a profit by bulk selling. The prices were so cheap that either her competitors perished or she acquired their businesses. Her motto is, “Sell cheap and tell the truth, don’t cheat nobody and don’t take no kickbacks.” It is the same business model that Amazon uses, i.e., making a profit by selling huge volumes of products.
